F3 SLC Archive Tools

A small personal project maintained by Rick Villela.

F3 SLC Archive Tools is a private utility that works with the F3 Salt Lake region's back-blast archive Gmail account. It is used by the account's administrator to:

  • Send a daily "On This Day" email highlighting back-blasts and photos posted on the same date in past years.
  • Build a searchable library of past back-blasts (workouts) for the region's leaders.

The app is not offered to the public. Access is limited to the archive account it was built for, and sign-in uses Google's standard OAuth consent screen.
