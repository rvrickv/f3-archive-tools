Privacy Policy

F3 SLC Archive Tools · Last updated October 12, 2026

What the app accesses

When you authorize F3 SLC Archive Tools with your Google account, it may read email messages and attachments in that account and send email on its behalf. It uses this access only to find back-blast emails and photos, build the archive library, and send the daily "On This Day" email.

How data is stored

Message content and photos the app processes are stored on a private server controlled by the app's maintainer. OAuth tokens are stored on that server only and are used solely to access the authorized account.

Sharing

Data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties. Archive content is only shown to recipients of the F3 Salt Lake region's archive emails and leaders using the library.

Google API Services

F3 SLC Archive Tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of stored data, email the contact below.
