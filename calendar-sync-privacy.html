Privacy Policy

F3 Calendar Sync · Last updated October 12, 2026

What the app accesses

When you authorize F3 Calendar Sync with your Google account, it can view and edit events on your calendars. It uses this access only to create, update and remove F3 Salt Lake region workout and event entries. It does not read email, contacts or other Google data.

How data is stored

Any schedule data and OAuth tokens the app uses are stored on a private server controlled by the app's maintainer and are used solely to keep the authorized calendar in sync.

Sharing

Data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

Google API Services

F3 Calendar Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of stored data, email the contact below.
